Our Story

At 231 Cuisine & Catering Services, every dish tells a story — a story of home, heritage, and heart.

Born from a simple dream to share the warmth, comfort, and bold flavors of Liberian and West African cuisine, 231 Cuisine has grown from humble home-cooked beginnings into a movement of love through food. What started as meals for friends and family has blossomed into a full culinary experience — one that celebrates culture, community, and connection.

Every pot of Jollof Rice, every bowl of Pepper Soup, and each sweet bite of Kala Donuts carries the essence of Liberia — a reminder of where we come from and a taste of who we are.

Our name, “231”, is more than a number. It’s the international calling code of Liberia — a symbol of pride, identity, and unity that connects us to our roots no matter where we are.

Whether it’s a wedding, birthday celebration, church event, or cultural festival, we bring the authentic taste of home to your table — seasoned with passion, prepared with love, and served with joy.

Come experience the flavor of Liberia — Your Homeland on a Plate.
