Our Story

Welcome to 231 Cuisine & Catering Services, based in Wilmington, Delaware. Our mission is to bring authentic Liberian flavors to your plate — from traditional soups and rice dishes to sweet pastries and snacks.

We offer dine-in, pickup, delivery, and catering for events. Every meal is cooked with love, passion, and pride for our Liberian roots.
